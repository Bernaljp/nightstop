## Part 1 — General

### 1.1 Applicability

This manual applies to all flight crew members employed by or contracted to HALCYON AIR
operating aircraft on the Air Operator Certificate. Where this manual is more
restrictive than the applicable regulation, this manual applies. Where the applicable
regulation is more restrictive, the regulation applies. Crew members in doubt as to
which applies shall contact Flight Operations before the duty commences.

### 1.2 Revision control

Revisions are issued electronically through the Crew Portal. Crew members are
responsible for confirming that they hold the current revision before each block of
duties. A revision bar in the right margin marks changed text. Temporary revisions are
issued as Flight Crew Notices and expire ninety days from issue unless incorporated.

### 1.3 Definitions

**Duty** — any task a crew member is required by the operator to carry out, including
flight duty, positioning, training, standby and administrative duties.

**Flight Duty Period (FDP)** — the period beginning when a crew member is required to
report for a duty that includes a sector and ending at on-blocks of the final sector.

**Report time** — the time at which a crew member is required to be available at the
designated reporting point, fit and ready for duty.

**Rest period** — a continuous and defined period of time during which a crew member is
free of all duties.

**Base** — the location nominated by the operator to the crew member from which the crew
member normally starts and ends a duty period.

**Positioning** — the transferring of a non-operating crew member from place to place at
the behest of the operator.

**Nightstop** — a rest period taken at a location other than base.

## Part 2 — Reporting and dispatch

### 2.1 Reporting point

The designated reporting point at base is the Crew Report Centre, Level 2, Terminal
Building. Away from base the reporting point is the hotel lobby unless the crew roster
states otherwise. Transport away from base is arranged by the operator and departs at
the time published in the crew transport schedule.

### 2.2 Reporting times

Crew members shall report at the times published on the roster. Where the roster does
not state a report time, the following apply, measured before scheduled time of
departure of the first sector:

- Short haul operations: 45 minutes
- Long haul operations: 75 minutes
- Positioning as a passenger: 60 minutes before scheduled departure

### 2.3 Fitness to operate

A crew member shall not report for duty if they know or suspect that they are suffering
from fatigue to a degree that may endanger the safety of the aircraft. A crew member
declaring unfit due to fatigue shall notify Crew Control at the earliest opportunity.
No disciplinary action follows from a fatigue declaration made in good faith.

### 2.4 Documentation

Crew members shall carry a valid licence, medical certificate, crew identity card and
passport with the minimum validity required by the destination. Failure to carry
required documentation resulting in a crew member being unable to operate is treated as
an absence.

## Part 3 — Uniform and personal presentation

### 3.1 Uniform standard

The uniform shall be clean, pressed and complete. The company tie or scarf is worn at
all times in the terminal building and during boarding. Epaulettes shall display the
correct number of bars for the crew member's rank.

### 3.2 Footwear

Black leather shoes with a closed toe and heel. Heel height shall not exceed 40mm for
operating crew. Trainers are not permitted in uniform at any time, including during
positioning.

### 3.3 Jewellery and visible tattoos

One ring per hand and one pair of plain stud earrings. Visible tattoos shall be covered
while in uniform. Facial piercings other than earrings are not permitted.

### 3.4 Baggage

Crew baggage shall be the company issue cabin case and flight bag. A crew member on a
nightstop of more than two nights may carry one additional soft bag not exceeding 10kg.

## Part 4 — Flight time limitations and rest

> **This is the part of the manual that matters for planning sleep.** It is four pages
> of a document that runs to several hundred.

### 4.1 Flight duty period limits

#### 4.1.1 Maximum daily flight duty period

The maximum FDP is determined by the table published in the applicable regulation, keyed
on report time and the number of sectors to be operated. Crew members shall satisfy
themselves before reporting that the planned FDP does not exceed the applicable maximum.

#### 4.1.2 Commander's discretion

The commander may extend an FDP beyond the planned limit where unforeseen circumstances
arise after the FDP has commenced. Any exercise of discretion shall be reported to
Flight Operations within 72 hours together with the reason and the extension taken.

#### 4.1.3 Company planning limit

**Notwithstanding the regulatory maximum, HALCYON AIR does not plan any flight duty
period beyond 13 hours.** A pairing that would require an FDP longer than 13 hours shall
not be published. Where operational disruption causes an FDP to exceed 13 hours, the
commander's discretion provisions of 4.1.2 apply and a fatigue report shall be filed.

### 4.2 Rest periods

#### 4.2.1 Minimum rest at base

**The minimum rest period at base is 12 hours**, measured from thirty minutes after
on-blocks of the final sector to the report time of the next duty. This is a company
standard and exceeds the regulatory minimum. Crew Planning shall not construct a roster
that provides less. Where disruption reduces actual rest below 12 hours at base, the
following duty shall be reallocated.

#### 4.2.2 Minimum rest away from base

The minimum rest period away from base is the length of the preceding duty period or ten
hours, whichever is the greater, plus travelling time between the airport and the
accommodation in both directions.

#### 4.2.3 Accommodation standard

Accommodation provided for a rest period shall have a bed in a quiet, dark, temperature
controlled room with access to food and drink. Crew members finding accommodation
falling short of this standard shall report it through the Crew Portal.

### 4.3 Duty patterns

#### 4.3.1 Days off

A crew member shall be given not fewer than eight days free of all duty in any calendar
month, of which at least two blocks shall be of two or more consecutive days.

#### 4.3.2 Consecutive early starts

**No crew member shall be rostered more than three consecutive duties reporting before
06:00 local time.** Crew Planning shall break a run of early starts with either a day
off or a duty reporting after 10:00 local. This limit exists because the sleep lost
before an early start accumulates across a run faster than any single night reveals, and
crew members consistently underestimate it.

#### 4.3.3 Transitions between early and late duties

Where a crew member transitions from a duty reporting after 16:00 local to a duty
reporting before 06:00 local, a minimum of 34 hours free of duty shall be provided
between them.

### 4.4 Standby

#### 4.4.1 Standby duty

A crew member on standby shall be contactable throughout the standby period and shall be
able to report within the notice period stated on the roster. Airport standby counts in
full towards duty. Home standby counts towards duty at 25 per cent until the crew member
is called.

#### 4.4.2 Notice period

The standard notice period from call to report is two hours for home standby and thirty
minutes for airport standby.

### 4.5 Fatigue reporting

Crew members shall file a fatigue report through the Crew Portal where they consider
that a duty, a pattern of duties or a rest period has resulted in a level of fatigue
that could affect safety. Reports are reviewed by the Fatigue Safety Action Group
monthly.

## Part 5 — Expenses and allowances

### 5.1 Duty allowance

A duty allowance is paid for each hour of duty performed away from base, at the rate
published in the Crew Pay Agreement and reviewed annually.

### 5.2 Meal allowance

A meal allowance is paid where a duty period exceeds five hours and no meal is provided.
Receipts are not required for amounts below the published threshold.

### 5.3 Expenses claims

Claims shall be submitted through the Crew Portal within sixty days of the duty to which
they relate. Claims submitted after sixty days will not be paid except where the crew
member was absent on sick leave for the whole period.

### 5.4 Currency and exchange

Allowances away from base are paid in the currency of the destination at the exchange
rate published on the first day of the month.

## Part 6 — Training and checking

### 6.1 Recurrent training

Each crew member shall complete recurrent training and checking within the periods
prescribed by the applicable regulation. The operator schedules recurrent training in
the crew member's roster and it counts as a duty for the purposes of Part 4.

### 6.2 Simulator sessions

A simulator session is a duty. Report time for a simulator session is the time stated on
the roster and there is no reporting offset — the crew member is due at the training
centre, not reporting for a flight.

### 6.3 Line checks

A line check is conducted on a normal revenue sector by a training captain. The crew
member is notified not less than seven days in advance.

### 6.4 Failure and retraining

A crew member failing a check is removed from line flying pending retraining. Retraining
is scheduled within fourteen days.

---

## Part 7 — Cabin safety and security

### 7.1 Pre-flight security check

A pre-flight security check of the flight deck shall be carried out before each
departure and recorded in the technical log.

### 7.2 Flight deck access

Flight deck access is restricted to operating crew and persons authorised under the
Operations Manual Part A. The flight deck door shall remain closed and locked from door
closure to door opening except as provided in the security procedures.

### 7.3 Dangerous goods

Crew members shall complete dangerous goods training as prescribed and shall not carry
prohibited articles in crew baggage.

---

## Part 8 — Administration

### 8.1 Rosters

Rosters are published not less than fourteen days before the start of the roster period.
A published roster may be changed by Crew Control with notice. Crew members shall check
the Crew Portal before each duty for changes.

### 8.2 Leave

Annual leave is bid for in the annual leave process. Leave is allocated by seniority
within each bid round.

### 8.3 Sickness

Crew members unfit for duty shall notify Crew Control as early as practicable and not
less than the notice period published in the Crew Pay Agreement.

### 8.4 Contact details

Crew members shall keep contact details current in the Crew Portal. Failure to be
contactable during a standby period is treated as an absence.
